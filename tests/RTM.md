- **MUST** — binding requirement; uncovered = P0 gap
- **SHOULD** — strongly recommended; uncovered = P1 (regulated overlay escalates to P0)
- **COULD** — nice-to-have; uncovered = P2 logged
- **WON'T** — explicitly out of scope; excluded from coverage math
